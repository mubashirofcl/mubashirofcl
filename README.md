## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I believe great web applications come from two things: **clean code** and a **strong focus on the user**.

As a MERN stack developer, I build responsive, fast, and intuitive digital products — from concept to deployment. I hold a **BCA from the University of Calicut** and have focused on **MongoDB, Express.js, React, and Node.js** as my core stack.

I'm constantly exploring new technologies, refining my DevOps workflow, and looking for opportunities to contribute to **real, meaningful projects** with teams that value quality and user-first thinking.

- 🔭 Currently working on **full-stack production applications** with cloud-native deployments
- 🌱 Learning **NestJS, TypeScript, and advanced AWS services**
- 💬 Ask me about **React, Node.js, MongoDB, Docker, or AWS deployments**
- ⚡ Fun fact: I enjoy tinkering with **Raspberry Pi** projects and exploring cybersecurity tools
